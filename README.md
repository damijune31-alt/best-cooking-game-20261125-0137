Ramen Rush — a ramen-shop cooking game. Orders fly onto the board, each one a
ticket for a bowl: one noodle base, one broth (Shoyu, Miso or Tonkotsu) and two or
three toppings. Walk the counter, pick ingredients one at a time, drop them into
the work bowl, and ring the bell to serve the finished bowl from the tray before
the customer's patience runs out. A 150-second shift, three lives, rising levels,
combos and tips for fast serves. Keyboard (arrows / A D, Space, S, P) or mouse
(click the counter to walk over and pick, drop or serve). All art and sounds are
drawn and synthesised in code; no assets, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Or hand the game in exactly as the grader does:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
